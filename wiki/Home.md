# 🧬 StaphScope - Welcome

Welcome to **StaphScope**, a species‑optimized computational pipeline for rapid and accessible *Staphylococcus aureus* genotyping and surveillance.

StaphScope integrates:

- **MLST** (Multi‑Locus Sequence Typing)
- **spa typing** (Protein A)
- **SCCmec typing** (Methicillin Resistance Cassette)
- **agr typing** (Accessory Gene Regulator – NEW in v1.3.0)
- **AMR gene detection** via AMRFinderPlus and ABRicate
- **Virulence gene screening** via VFDB
- **BACMET** (Biocide & Heavy Metal Resistance)
- **Plasmid replicon detection**
- **Point mutation detection**
